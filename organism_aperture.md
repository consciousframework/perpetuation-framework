## Part I. Scope and Vocabulary

### What this document houses

This document instantiates the Seed's architecture at the organism order. It covers:

- The organism as an entity: its template, constituent dimensions, and boundary dimension.
- Its constitutive and integrative dimensions.
- Registration, representation, and the choosability that representation opens.
- The boundary's relational dynamics: emission, permeability, and their pairings.
- Messenger-mediated coupling and its topologies.
- Nested representation and modeled perspectives.
- Resonance and its genuine and false forms.
- Criteria drift, disclosure, catalysis, re-disclosure, and control.
- Cooperation and consent.
- Cross-scale dynamics.
- Failure modes.

Where it relies on a mechanism of the Seed, it restates that mechanism in the terms it needs. It is organism-order specific.

### Tier discipline

**[Tier 1]:** Confirmed biology, physiology, or physics independently measurable whether or not the framework exists.

**[Tier 2]:** Framework vocabulary applied by structural correspondence. Scaffold mapping. Correspondence illuminates; it cannot confirm.

**[Tier 3]:** Process projections, trajectory schemas, behavioral correlates or relational typologies not directly checkable at this order.

### Vocabulary

**Constitutive dimensions** are higher-order dimensions that emerge from cooperation among lower-order entities (cells, tissues, organs, organ systems). The criteria that govern them are genuine by emergence, and the configurations realized are sound to the degree the constituents' internal coherence is.

**Integrative dimensions** are dimensions the organism gains through dimensional integration. They are where genuineness must be disclosed through exploration.

**The boundary dimension** is the emergent dimension representing how the organism constrains and is constrained by external territory. Its positions are pairs: (configuration, interaction profile).

**Internal coherence criteria (ICC)** are the criteria from which the organism resolves its positions. They may remain aligned with, or diverge from, the Genuine Coherence Criteria.

**Emission and permeability** are the two settings of the organism's ICC in a coupling: how it constrains the other entity, and how much incoming constraint it admits.

**Direct mutual coupling** is a coupling in which a single Genuine Coherence Criterion constrains both entities' boundaries simultaneously.

**Messenger-mediated coupling** is a coupling in which constraint travels through one or more intermediate entities. Bidirectionality applies within each link, not across the chain.

**Chained topology** is a messenger-mediated coupling with no return path. Constraint flows in one direction. The observed entity does not register the observer.

**Mutual topology** is a messenger-mediated coupling in which a return path closes the loop. Each entity's behavior constrains the other's behavior under the same criterion.

**Nested representation** is the organism-order capacity to construct models of other entities' models, including models of itself as seen by another. It is built from the organism's own ICC and registrations, not from direct access to the other's internal state.

**Resonance** is the experiential name for the alignment of positions across entities in a coupling. It is the felt or observed correlate of balanced coupling, coherent configuration, or cooperation. It does not imply genuineness. False resonance arises from control or complementarity of imbalance.

**Conditioning** is criteria drift plus resistance to re-disclosure. A drifted criterion that has not been locked can still re-resolve under contact; a conditioned one resists.

**Limit-setting** is the human practice of holding limits with others. It is called limit-setting here to avoid confusion with the boundary dimension.

## Part II. The Organism as Entity

### The aperture template

The organism is an entity: an instantiation of an aperture template at the organism order. Its aperture is constituted by a set of dimensions whose positions are mutually constrained by Genuine Coherence Criteria, and the mutual constraint closes into a loop. [Tier 1: the organism as a bounded, self-maintaining system. Tier 2: the framework mapping.]

The template fixes a subset of dimensions as stable positions (species-typical architecture, organ-system modules, genetic inheritance) and leaves another subset open to constrained variation (calibrated allostasis, developmental record, learning history, relational calibration). [Tier 1: inherited versus accumulated structure. Tier 2: the framework mapping.]

### The Self-Referential Instrument

The organism resolves its dimensional positions from its ICC. It does not compare incoming variation against a fixed position. It asks whether the variation produces internal changes coherent with its existing dimensional structure, or whether it produces contradiction. Its reference is the criterion itself, not a position. Positions vary within the constraints of the criteria, and the criteria are what persist. [Tier 2]

The organism is the same kind of entity as at every other order. What differs is the range of possibilities available to it: representation, individual-level exploration, relational calibration. The mechanism of resolution is the same.

### The Interiority Principle

The organism navigates only the internal variations produced in it by external territory acting across its boundary through at least one shared Genuine Coherence Criterion. Its Self-Referential Instrument operates on this internal registration substrate. [Tier 1: sensory and interoceptive systems register internal states, not external objects. Tier 2: the framework mapping.]

### Finitude

The organism's capacity is limited by metabolic constraints, sensory bandwidth, cognitive load, relational bandwidth, and temporal horizon. Because capacity is finite, the organism cannot hold, process, or change positions across all dimensions at once. Finitude forces compression and constrained organization. [Tier 1: metabolic and cognitive limits. Tier 2: the framework mapping.]

## Part III. The Organism's Dimensions

### Constitutive dimensions

Constitutive dimensions emerge from cooperation among lower-order entities, whose boundaries are mutually constrained by Genuine Coherence Criteria. [Tier 1: organismal integration of organ systems. Tier 2: the framework mapping.]

- **Somatic allostasis:** adaptive regulation of organism state under changing demand, from the cooperation of metabolic, cardiovascular, respiratory, and endocrine systems.
- **Behavioral autonomy:** locomotion, perceptual exploration, and self-directed movement, from musculoskeletal, nervous, and sensory systems.
- **Ecological navigation:** energy acquisition, predation avoidance, and thermal management, from sensory, motor, and metabolic systems.

The criteria governing these dimensions are genuine by emergence. The configurations realized are sound to the degree the constituents' internal coherence is, so drift in cells or tissues can degrade them. [Tier 2]

### Integrative dimensions

When the organism is coupled to an external dimension through Genuine Coherence Criteria but cannot sustain internal coherence under the full spectrum of that dimension's positions, it integrates the dimension. It incorporates internal mechanisms that produce the positioning, gains the capacity to navigate those variations from its own resolution process, and prevents capacity-limit failure. Integration adds the dimension to its constituent set, so the boundary dimension reorganizes. [Tier 2]

- **Thermal regulation:** sweating, shivering, vasodilation, and vasoconstriction produce positions on the temperature dimension. [Tier 1]
- **Ion regulation:** renal and hormonal mechanisms produce positions on the ionic concentration dimension. [Tier 1]
- **Immune discrimination:** central tolerance, clonal selection, and inflammatory response produce positions on the molecular pattern dimension. [Tier 1]

Each has a pre-existing genuine criterion that must be disclosed through exploration. In these three, most of the exploration was done at lineage level, and the individual calibrates what it inherited. At the representational layer, exploration also happens within the individual.

### The boundary dimension

The boundary dimension emerges as the organism navigates its constituent dimensions coherently. Each position pairs a complete configuration of the constituent dimensions with the coupling capacity that configuration confers: which Genuine Coherence Criteria can act on the organism, and how. [Tier 2]

Internally, the boundary is the organism's binding element and represents its current state. Externally, it is the organism's interaction profile: the component through which Genuine Coherence Criteria constrain the organism together with external territory. Integrating a dimension extends the boundary's position space.

**Behavior as boundary position.** Behavior is the observable, external expression of the current configuration. It is the interaction profile being enacted: the smile, the gaze, the posture, the tone, the movement. The internal configuration is private. Behavior is what other entities can register. When a Genuine Coherence Criterion acts across the organism's boundary, behavior is the carrier of the outgoing constraint. It is not the constraint itself; the constraint is the effect of the behavior under the criterion on the other entity's boundary. [Tier 2]

### Two layers of criteria

The organism's ICC operate on two layers.

The **regulatory layer** holds set points and regulatory relations: temperature, osmolarity, blood pressure. It is largely inherited and recalibrated physiologically. In fever, pyrogens raise the thermoregulatory set point, and a person with a rising fever feels cold in a warm room. The same input resolves differently under updated criteria. [Tier 1: fever physiology. Tier 2: the mapping.]

The **representational layer** holds expectations about territory, other entities, relationships, and the self. It is acquired and revised through experience. This is where criteria can be installed, and where most of the questions of this document concentrate. [Tier 2]

The two layers interact. Regulatory drift constrains what the representational layer can resolve, because a dysregulated physiology limits the states available for representation to work with. And representational drift can override regulatory signals, as when chronic stress sustains a set point that the organism's representational criteria would otherwise resolve away. The layers are distinguishable but not independent.

## Part IV. The Boundary's Relational Dynamics

### Incoming and outgoing constraint

A Genuine Coherence Criterion acting across the organism's boundary constrains both parties. From the organism's side, the incoming constraint requires changes in its configuration, resolved against its ICC. The outgoing constraint requires changes in the other entity.

A party **honors** a constraint when it re-resolves in response to the other's change in the proportion the criterion prescribes. In a genuine coupling, both parties honor, so when one changes position, the other follows, and vice versa. Asymmetries lie in capacity and magnitude, not in the direction of constraint. [Tier 2]

### Direct and messenger-mediated coupling

A coupling between two organisms can take two forms.

**Direct mutual coupling** occurs when a single Genuine Coherence Criterion constrains both organisms' boundaries simultaneously. The constraint is bidirectional by structure: each party's position constrains the other's, and the loop closes.

**Messenger-mediated coupling** occurs when constraint travels through one or more intermediate entities. Bidirectionality applies within each link, not across the chain. An organism and a photon are directly coupled; the photon and another organism are directly coupled; but no single criterion constrains both organisms at once. [Tier 2]

Most organism-order couplings are messenger-mediated. Vision travels through photons. Hearing travels through pressure waves. Smell travels through molecules. Touch travels through mechanical contact. The framework treats each link as a direct coupling and the chain as a composite topology.

### Chained and mutual topology

Messenger-mediated coupling has two topologies.

**Chained topology:** no return path exists. Constraint flows in one direction. Individual A looks at individual B. Photons reflect from B and reach A. A registers B and resolves a representation of B. B registers photon impacts, but those registrations do not encode A's state. B does not register A. The relation is asymmetric at the level of representation. [Tier 2]

**Mutual topology:** a return path closes the loop. Individual B turns and looks at A. Now photons travel from B to A and from A to B. Each registers the other's behavior. Each resolves a representation of the other. The loop closes. Each organism's behavior is now an outgoing constraint on the other's behavior under the same criterion. [Tier 2]

The physical mediators remain in both topologies. What changes is the constraint structure: chained has no return path; mutual has one. The transition from chained to mutual is what makes mutual perception, joint attention, and social coordination possible.

### Two settings per coupling

In each coupling, the organism's ICC set two things.

**Emission** is how the organism's boundary constrains the other entity.

- *Accurate* emission expresses the organism's state in the proportion the criterion prescribes.
- *Imposing* emission demands more following than the criterion prescribes and does not honor the return.
- *Withholding* emission masks or suppresses the organism's state, so the other entity cannot follow accurately.

**Permeability** is how much incoming constraint the organism admits.

- *Selective* permeability admits positions that survive a test against the organism's own contradictions, and rejects those that do not.
- *Sealed* permeability rejects incoming positions regardless of coherence.
- *Porous* permeability admits them without testing.

Both are settings of integrative dimensions, which the organism resolves. They can therefore be genuine or drifted. Selective permeability and accurate emission are the genuine forms: the organism's ICC are aligned with the Genuine Coherence Criteria, and its integrative dimensions admit positions and set limits accordingly. Imposing, withholding, sealed, and porous are the forms these settings take when the ICC are not genuine.

The settings belong to couplings, not to organisms. The same organism may be selective in one relationship and porous in another. [Tier 2]

### Balanced settings and how they are identified

A balanced coupling is one in which both parties hold accurate emission and selective permeability. When both parties hold these settings, the relationship closes without persistent cost to either.

"Persistent cost" has independent markers, and they can be named without circularity. Persistent cost is present when:

- A registration is suppressed with no outlet.
- One party absorbs a constraint the other declines to honor.
- The relationship closes only because one party pays continuously for it to close.

Balance is the setting-pair in which these markers are absent. It is not defined by their absence; it is defined by the settings, and their absence is its consequence.

Balance is not equal strength. Where capacities differ—as between caregiver and child, or teacher and learner—the party with more capacity absorbs more of the adjustment. Control reverses this: the party with more capacity absorbs less. [Tier 2/3]

### Resonance and its forms

**Resonance** is the alignment of positions across entities in a coupling. It is the experiential name for what the framework describes structurally as balanced coupling, coherent configuration, or cooperation, depending on context. It is what individuals feel and have named, even when they cannot explain it. Using the term connects the framework to lived vocabulary. [Tier 3 experiential]

Resonance is an end state, not a cause. The same alignment can arise from genuine or non-genuine ICC.

- **Genuine resonance.** Both parties hold accurate emission and selective permeability. Their ICC are genuine. The relationship closes without persistent cost. Each party's positions track the Genuine Coherence Criteria. This is the experiential correlate of balanced coupling.

- **False resonance.** At least one party's ICC are drifted. The alignment is produced by control (imposing/sealed paired with withholding/porous) or by complementarity of imbalance (each confirms the other's drift). From the inside, it can feel the same as genuine resonance. It is distinguished by persistent cost, narrowing of navigable positions, and resistance to re-disclosure. [Tier 2/3]

At the representational threshold, resonance is mediated by each organism's internal model. Each entity builds a model from its own ICC, resolves positions from that model, and emits behavior. The alignment of behaviors is what each registers. The models remain private. What feels like resonance is the alignment of positions, not access to the other's model.

Resonance does not imply genuineness. This is the same distinction the framework already draws between end state and cause: an observed alignment does not identify the setting that produced it.

### End state versus cause

An observed outcome is an end state, and the same end state can have different causes. The Seed's three outcomes describe end states: the entity preserves its ICC, installs external criteria, or updates and recovers coherence. The settings above describe causes, and different settings can produce one end state. [Tier 2]

- **Preserved ICC.** With genuine ICC, a selective organism tests the demand against its contradictions, finds it incoherent, and an integrative dimension sets a limit accordingly. With drifted ICC, a sealed organism rejects the demand, or rejects a catalytic signal that would have let it re-disclose. The end state is the same. The first closes without persistent cost; the second does not.
- **Changed ICC.** A selective organism may adopt a criterion after running it through its own contradictions and finding it more accurate. That is disclosure, and its ICC become more genuine. A porous organism adopts the criterion untested, which is installation. An organism that depends on the relationship may adopt it at persistent cost, which looks like adoption and is the following pole of control.

An observer therefore cannot read the cause from the end state. What separates the causes is whether the organism's own resolution was run, whether its ICC were aligned with the Genuine Coherence Criteria before and after, and whether the relationship closes without persistent cost.

The typology of imposing, withholding, sealed, and porous names how couplings look when ICC are not genuine. A genuine organism can reject or update and remain selective. [Tier 2]

### Pairings and outcomes

| Emitting side | Receiving side | Outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Accurate | Selective, criteria genuine | Genuine cooperation. Constitutive dimensions emerge. |
| Accurate | Selective, criteria drifted | Catalysis. The receiver tests the signaled positions through its own resolution and may disclose the genuine criterion. |
| Accurate | Porous | The signal is absorbed untested. Installation if the emitter's criteria are drifted; dependence if they are genuine. |
| Accurate | Sealed | Rejection. The emitter's coherent state is not honored; no closure. |
| Imposing | Porous | Installation. With the imposer sealed to the return on the same coupling, this is control. |
| Imposing | Selective | The receiver tests the imposition and rejects what fails; no closure, and the imposer's criteria are contradicted. |
| Imposing | Sealed | Collision. No closure; both parties pay cost. |
| Withholding | Any | The receiver lacks accurate information and cannot follow; no closure, and the withholder's suppressed registration persists as cost. |

The same accurate signal is catalytic to a selective receiver and installing to a porous one. What separates catalysis from installation is the receiver's permeability, not the signal. [Tier 2]

### The mirror pairing

The imposing, sealed configuration and the withholding, porous configuration are mirror images, and each supplies what the other's criteria need.

The derivation runs as follows.

An imposing pole demands more following than the criterion prescribes. Such a demand cannot be sustained in a coupling where the other party tests it. A selective receiver tests the demand against its own contradictions, finds it incoherent, and rejects it. The imposing pole therefore needs a receiver that does not test. That is a porous receiver.

A porous receiver admits positions without testing. It cannot generate criteria from its own resolution, because it does not run its own resolution. It needs a source whose criteria it can adopt. That is an imposing source.

So the two poles need each other. The imposing pole needs non-testing; the porous pole needs supply. Each side's drift is confirmed by the other's, and neither encounters the contradiction that would challenge it. The pairing is therefore stable.

The cost is borne mostly by the following pole, but the sealed pole also pays. Its sealed permeability shuts the return constraint that would reveal its own drift. It cannot be contradicted, and it therefore cannot be corrected. [Tier 2/3]

### The balanced configuration and what it requires

A balanced organism is selective in permeability and accurate in emission in the coupling at hand. Three conditions follow from the architecture. [Tier 2]

- **Toward the unknown.** Disclosure is active exploration, so the organism must be able to hold contradictions open without installing a criterion early. This is a capacity condition, not waiting. It has a cost, and an organism without spare capacity or support cannot meet it.
- **Toward other entities.** The organism keeps the other's resolution intact. Installation bypasses it and produces drift; catalysis does not. Consent is the human form: the other's resolution process stays in charge of what it adopts.
- **Toward its own criteria.** The organism holds them revisable. An installed criterion resolves accurately and presents as the organism's own, so no organism can certify its own genuineness from inside. Genuineness is checked by convergence from independent entities and by the cost signature. This is scaffold-holding applied to one's own criteria.

The step from these conditions to a norm is separate. The framework says what preserves coherence; valuing coherence is an additional premise. [Tier 3]

### The conditioned configuration

A conditioned configuration is drifted and resists re-disclosure. It is relational in origin and emergence: the same organism may be conditioned in one relationship and not in another.

The resistance has two sources. The organism is its pattern of criteria, so exposing a criterion as not genuine threatens its own coherence. And the following pole gave up integrative coherence to protect a constitutive dimension it depends on—the relationship itself—so re-disclosure puts that dimension at risk. A catalytic signal is therefore read as a contradiction to defend against, and it is met with sealed permeability. [Tier 2/3]

Behavioral correlates, such as seeking control, urgency, or stabilizing on a criterion to avoid the friction of exploration, follow plausibly from drift but are not entailed by the architecture. [Tier 3]

## Part V. Registration, Representation and Choosability

### Registration and representation

Every entity registers internal variations produced by encounter. The organism order adds **representation**: a stable, reusable correlation structure, built from the organism's registrations, that can be navigated without immediate input. It holds correlations with external entities, with the organism itself, and with its relationships. [Tier 1: memory, learning, and prediction in nervous systems. Tier 2: the mapping.]

Representation brings capacities no lower order has: anticipating, comparing alternatives before acting, learning across time, and modeling other entities and itself. The sensorimotor loop closes within one entity, which then carries a model of the loop it is in. [Tier 2]

### Choosability

Representation lets the organism hold alternative positions apart from immediate input, and that is what makes choice possible. **Choosability** is the capacity to permit or withhold disclosure: to re-explore a criterion against its own contradictions, or to hold it against the contradiction. It is not the capacity to select criteria from a menu. The organism always resolves from its own ICC; what it chooses is whether those ICC can update.

Much installation happens without any choice, as when conditioning occurs in early life. Choosability is available to the organism, not guaranteed. [Tier 2]

### Three sources of inaccuracy

A represented territory is a correlation, so it can be inaccurate. Three sources need to be kept apart. [Tier 2]

- **Partial.** Finitude and limited channels mean the organism couples to only a subset of a source's dimensions. This is incompleteness, not failure.
- **Damaged.** The resolution mechanism is degraded: loss of fidelity.
- **Drifted.** The criteria themselves were installed without disclosure: loss of criteria integrity.

An illusion produced by genuine criteria under unusual input is partial, not drifted. Without this separation, drift becomes a catch-all.

### Exploration moves to the individual

At the cell order, exploration of a new dimension happens at lineage level, through variation and selection. At the organism order, it happens within the individual, through learning, play, and trial, and in humans across culture as well. That shift is why an organism can drift, and recalibrate, within one lifetime. [Tier 2]

Exploration has a cost: holding contradictions open consumes capacity. That cost is why installation is cheap, and why conditioning, which blocks exploration, can persist.

### Reach of integration

The organism can integrate any dimension its channels couple to, including channels extended by instruments and culture, within the bounds of its template and capacity. A species' sensory channels fix which dimensions it can integrate: a bee and a human couple to different subsets of the same light. [Tier 1: species differ in photoreceptor sensitivities. Tier 2: the mapping.]

### Relational calibration

In the representational slice, the organism integrates the relational trust dimension. Attachment calibration, limit-setting, and consent negotiation produce positions on the trust axis. As an integrative dimension, its genuineness must be disclosed through exploration: occupying different positions, meeting contradictions, and resolving the criterion through them. If the organism stabilizes on an installed criterion without that exploration, the dimension is drifted. [Tier 2]

### Nested representation and modeled perspectives

At the representational threshold, the organism can construct models of other entities' models, including models of itself as seen by another. This capacity follows from representation and choosability, and it is what makes social coordination, joint attention, and self-perception possible without introducing consciousness as a primitive. [Tier 2]

The structure is a hierarchy of registrations and constructions. Consider two individuals, A and B, in mutual topology.

1. **B's actual internal state.** B's configuration, resolved from B's ICC. A has no direct access to it.
2. **A's registration of B's behavior.** A registers B's gaze, posture, expression. This is directly supported by territory-mediated variation. It is a registration, not a model of B's interior.
3. **A's model of what B represents.** A constructs a model of B's internal representation from A's own ICC and representational structure. This is inference, not access.
4. **A's model of A-as-seen-by-B.** A constructs a model of its own state under the inferred perspective of B. This is a self-model. It is generated from A's own ICC and representational structure.

Only #2 is directly registered. #3 and #4 are constructed. They can diverge from B's actual internal state without either A or B being incoherent. The divergence is a consequence of representational asymmetry and finite access. It is partial representation, not damaged or drifted. Drift would enter if the criteria A uses to generate the model were installed without disclosure—for example, if A assumes B sees A as confident because a caregiver installed that criterion, and A never tested it against its own contradictions.

**Models are not entities.** A representation is an internal correlation structure. It is not an instantiation of an aperture template at its own order. It has no boundary dimension in the Seed's sense. Genuine Coherence Criteria act across the boundaries of entities, through messenger entities or direct coupling. They do not act between two entities' internal models. There are no Genuine Coherence Criteria between models. When two models appear incoherent—A represents a warm day, B represents coats and umbrellas—the incoherence is not between the models. It is between each model and the territory, or between the two entities' positions on a shared dimension. The apparent contradiction is a diagnostic signal, not evidence of a coupling between models. [Tier 2]

**Self-perception is not perception of the self as an external object.** It is the organism's representation of its own state under a modeled external perspective. ICC supplies the criteria. The representational mechanism constructs the model. The model can contain the self as represented under those criteria. This capacity is restricted to the representational slice: social mammals and humans, to different degrees. Sub-threshold organism mechanics—thermoregulation, allostasis, immune function—are order-general and automatic, and do not instantiate nested representation. [Tier 2 with Tier 3 caveat on species distribution]

### The representational slice

Representation, and with it choosability and nested representation, applies to a specific slice of the organism order: social mammals and humans, to different degrees. Sub-threshold organism mechanics—thermoregulation, allostasis, immune function—are order-general and automatic. [Tier 2]

## Part VI. Criteria Drift, Disclosure and Control

### Criteria drift

Internal coherence criteria may drift from Genuine Coherence Criteria, gradually or suddenly. Drift originates in how the organism resolves an integrative dimension against the rest of its existing dimensional structure.

To disclose the genuine criterion for a new axis, the organism varies positions along it and finds which combinations with positions on its existing dimensions contradict. The criterion is what resolves those contradictions across the full range of the structure's positions. A drifted criterion also fits the existing structure, but by restriction: it avoids or suppresses the positions that would contradict it. The navigable space narrows, and the contradictions persist as cost.

A criterion resolved against a structure that already carries drift will be coherent with that structure. It is still not genuine. Drift can therefore compound. Genuineness needs an anchor outside the structure: convergence from independent entities, and the cost signature. As drift increases, resolution becomes increasingly costly and depletes finite capacity. [Tier 2]

### Three routes to a criterion

- **Own exploration.** The organism proposes positions along the axis, meets contradictions among occupied positions under its existing ICC, and resolves the criterion through them. The criterion is disclosed.
- **Catalysis.** Another organism that has already resolved the criterion signals positions to test. The organism resolves them against its own contradictions through its own process before adopting the criterion. The criterion is disclosed by the organism, not supplied to it.
- **Installation.** The organism stabilizes on a criterion without resolving it against its own contradictions. Its own resolution is bypassed.

The routes differ in who proposes the positions and whether the organism's own resolution is kept. Disclosure costs capacity; installation is cheap. [Tier 2]

### Re-disclosure

Since integrative dimensions are resolved by the organism's own mechanisms, a drifted dimension can be re-resolved by disclosing the genuine criterion, which replaces the distorted one.

Re-disclosure is a process, not an event. It needs remaining capacity, an environment that supports re-exploration, and positions the organism can test through its own resolution. It usually needs repetition. Whether conditioning can be erased completely, or leaves a trace, is left open. [Tier 2]

### The library of resolved patterns

Genuine exploration accumulates. Each disclosure leaves the organism with a resolved pattern: a criterion it can recognize when it appears again, and a contradiction it can localize when it reappears.

The accumulated library is what lets the organism diagnose later encounters. When a coupling fails, the organism can match the failure against the resolved patterns it holds. If the failure matches, the organism can localize the resistance and identify its source. If it does not match, and persists across varied positions, the organism has evidence that the resistance lies where it has not yet explored.

Conditioning narrows the space of navigable positions and depletes the library of resolved patterns. An organism with a depleted library cannot localize resistance, cannot identify causes, and cannot distinguish between a genuine incompatibility and its own drift. This is one reason recovery from conditioning requires repetition and supporting conditions, not a single event. [Tier 2]

### Control

**Control** is the sustained pairing, on one coupling, of an imposing and sealed pole with a withholding and porous pole. The controlling pole does not honor the return constraint, and the following pole absorbs both its own load and the share the controlling pole declines. [Tier 2]

The following pole continues to resolve from its own ICC. The Self-Referential Instrument is never replaced. What changes is the provenance of the criteria it references: they now include criteria it did not resolve against its own contradictions. From inside, installed criteria are indistinguishable from disclosed ones, so conditioning is hard to detect by introspection. The signal is behavioral: vary positions, test conditions, and see whether the criteria can update. Resistance to updating is the marker, along with persistent cost.

Control acts directly only on integrative dimensions, because only there do an entity's criteria set how it responds. Constitutive dimensions emerge from the boundary positions of constituents and cannot be bent by any one of them. But control can act on constitutive dimensions indirectly. If a constituent's ICC are drifted, its contribution to the constitutive dimension is distorted, and the higher-order configuration degrades through that constituent. The control is still on the constituent's integrative dimensions; the effect propagates upward through the loop.

A child whose cry is not answered is an example. The cry is a genuine outgoing constraint. The caregiver does not honor it. The child's outgoing channel is suppressed, and its incoming permeability rises. The registration persists with no outlet, and suppressing it is the persistent cost. [Tier 3: a structural illustration, not a developmental claim.]

Deliberate manufacturing of criteria is a modifier, not the definition. Most control transmits the controlling pole's own drifted criteria and involves no design. Where a party designs criteria to install, the pairing is the same and the responsibility differs.

Control is not the same as asymmetry of capacity. A party with more capacity that absorbs more of the adjustment is cooperating; one that absorbs less is controlling.

## Part VII. Cooperation at the Organism Order

Cooperation occurs when Genuine Coherence Criteria mutually constrain multiple organisms' boundaries in a coherent relationship, and the configurations resulting from the relationship satisfy each organism's ICC. Higher-order dimensions emerge from the organisms' boundary positions and cannot be navigated by any constituent alone. The loop closes, the apertures expand, and a new order begins. [Tier 2]

Cooperation requires that both organisms are accurate in emission and selective in permeability, so each honors the other's constraints in the proportion their capacities allow. Where criteria are conditioned, the pairing falls into imposing and following, and the relationship is not cooperation. Cooperation is not agreement, compromise, coordination, aggregation, or redundancy. It founds a new order or expands the constitutive dimensions of an existing aperture.

**Consent** is the relational expression of balanced settings: each party's resolution stays in charge of what it adopts, so the Genuine Coherence Criterion acts across both boundaries without either imposing or submitting. Where consent is absent, at least one party is imposing, withholding, sealed, or porous in that coupling, and the relationship is not genuine. [Tier 2]

## Part VIII. Cross-Scale Dynamics

The loop is one, expanded vertically. The organism's constitutive dimensions are the coherent expression of the same closed cycle that holds cells, tissues, and organs together, and each constituent's own loop is nested inside it as a module. Because positions at every order are mutually constrained within the expanded loop, a loss of coherence at one order alters what the others can resolve, in both directions, through boundary positions. [Tier 2]

**Top-down.** When the organism's ICC drift, its signals to lower-order modules become contradictory. The modules read them as contradiction against their own criteria and preserve those criteria by closing channels. This is the preserving outcome applied to a higher-order source. [Tier 2]

**Bottom-up.** When a module loses genuine coherence, it keeps local stability by closing channels to signals the organism's aperture needs. Viewed from the module, it is local constraint. Viewed from the organism, it is loss of integration. [Tier 2]

**Sacrifice.** The converse also occurs. An entity may give up local coherence to protect a higher-order dimension it depends on. This is what the following pole does in control: it gives up integrative coherence to preserve a constitutive dimension it depends on, the relationship itself. [Tier 3]

How far effects propagate depends on each constituent's capacity and ICC. Because higher-order positions are functions of the interaction-profile component, propagation should occur only through changes in the profile. A lower-order change that leaves the profile intact should not register above. [Tier 3]

## Part IX. Failure Modes at the Organism Order

**Capacity-limit failure.** The organism cannot occupy a coherent position because its finitude is exceeded, and the configuration is destroyed. This classifies contact, not coherence: the criteria may be genuine and the mechanism accurate, and the perturbation still exceeds structural limits. [Tier 1: death by trauma, starvation, extreme exposure. Tier 2: the mapping.]

**Loss of fidelity.** The coupling between the position the ICC prescribe and the position the organism resolves degrades. It is a property of the resolution mechanism, not of the criteria. It has two etiologies.

- **Material:** mechanism degradation, pathogen attack, sensor decay, overwhelming perturbation. The remedy is repair. [Tier 1 for the examples.]
- **Systemic:** contradictory signaling from lower-order modules. The remedy is restoring coherent signaling. [Tier 2]

**Loss of coherence criteria integrity.** The ICC drift from the Genuine Coherence Criteria, with the rising cost and capacity depletion of drift. Loss of fidelity requires repair of the mechanism; loss of criteria integrity requires re-disclosure.

Conditioning is the form of drift that resists re-disclosure. It narrows the space of navigable positions and depletes the library of resolved patterns. Recovery needs repetition and supporting conditions, not a single event. [Tier 2]

Partial perception is not a failure mode. It is the normal condition of any entity coupled through a subset of a source's dimensions.

## Part XI. Epistemic Posture

### Territory agnosticism

This document does not require a settled answer to what territory is prior to encounter. Territory is the universe of existing dimensional variations and the Genuine Coherence Criteria constraining them. The organism navigates only its internal registrations of territory.

### The order limitation

The organism, at the organism order, cannot confirm what the next order's transition actually requires. It also cannot reliably distinguish developments that constitute the leading edge of that transition from developments that merely resemble it.

### Two senses of scaffold

**Scaffold-holding** is a stance toward one's own compression: genuinely held, genuinely revisable, genuinely current. **Scaffold as an epistemic status** names a claim implied by the architecture but not yet independently checkable.

### The current status

The architecture described here has not yet failed a check it was run against. That is the entirety of the claim this document is prepared to stand behind. It is a scaffold, and it welcomes coherent additions from any dimension, provided they survive the same check applied everywhere else: would this addition still hold if generated fresh against what is actually here?
