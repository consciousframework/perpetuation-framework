# Perpetuation Framework

A tiered, falsifiable scaffold describing how coherence is maintained, transmitted, and lost across orders of organization — from atoms to molecules to cells to organisms to computing systems.

---

## What this repository is

This repository houses the **Perpetuation Framework** (PF): a structural architecture that describes how entities at different orders resolve their positions under coherence criteria, how they couple, cooperate, drift, and fail, and how coherence propagates across orders through nested loops.

The framework is presented as a **scaffold**, not a settled theory. Every claim is tier-tagged. Every significant claim has a falsifiability condition. The framework explicitly welcomes disconfirmation.

## What this repository is not

- It is not a claim to have explained consciousness, life, or intelligence.
- It is not a physical theory that competes with existing physics, chemistry, or biology.
- It is not a normative framework that says how entities *should* behave.
- It is not finished.

The framework maps existing substrate claims (physics, chemistry, biology, computing) onto a structural vocabulary so that the structure can be tested across orders. Where it adds to what the substrate sciences already say, it does so as classification, not as physical mechanism.

---

## The architecture in brief

The framework describes:

- **Dimensions** — axes of variation.
- **Coherence criteria** — the rules that describe how positions across dimensions can coexist without contradiction.
- **Genuine Coherence Criteria (GCC)** — coherence criteria existing as structural law.
- **Apertures and templates** — the space of coherent configurations, and the classes of coherent configurations that entities instantiate.
- **Entities** — instantiations of aperture templates, resolving positions from their own Internal Coherence Criteria (ICC).
- **Boundaries** — emergent dimensions representing how an entity constrains and is constrained by external territory. Boundary positions are pairs: (configuration, interaction profile).
- **Registration and resolution** — registration is the automatic production of internal variation when a GCC acts across a boundary; resolution is the entity's ICC-dependent determination of its position.
- **Dimensional integration** — horizontal expansion of an entity's aperture through internalizing a formerly external dimension.
- **Cooperation** — mutual constraint of multiple entities' boundaries under GCCs, producing relationally irreducible higher-order dimensions (constitutive dimensions).
- **Modularization** — the process by which an entity becomes a reusable constituent of a higher-order aperture.
- **Drift, adoption, and control** — failure modes of coherence criteria at orders that have integrative dimensions and exploration.
- **Cross-scale dynamics** — coherence propagation through nested loops, in both directions.

The architecture is **recursive**. The same generative grammar operates at every order. The loop is one, expanded vertically, with each constituent's own loop nested within it as a module.

---

## The parallel ladders

The framework describes **two ladders of complexity**, both emerging through the same generativity process from different material substrates:

- The **biological ladder**: atoms → molecules → macromolecules → supramolecular assemblies → prokaryotic cells → eukaryotic cells → tissues → organs → organisms.
- The **computational ladder**: computational constituents → computational higher-order structures → the computing-sequence order (AI).

The ladders are **lateral**, not nested. Cross-scale dynamics between the AI and the human user are lateral coupling between orders, not vertical nesting of one within the other.
